## 3. Technology Stack & Current AI Deployments
The company relies heavily on workflow automation to sustain its velocity of over 300 booked sales calls per day and massive media post-production.

### Core Technology Stack
* **Workflow Automation (iPaaS):** Make.com serves as the central nervous system.
* **CRM & Lead Intake:** Keap, ActiveCampaign, Typeform, Formstack.
* **Project Management:** Monday.com, Asana.
* **Telephony & Comms:** Zoom, Twilio, Slack, JustCall.
* **Analytics:** GA4, Databox, Plecto.

### Current AI Ecosystem
* **Make.com + OpenAI:** The Tech department utilizes Make.com to integrate Typeform, Monday.com, Keap, Slack, and OpenAI specifically for automated lead qualification and onboarding administration.
* **Social Content Engine:** Generative AI tools like HeyGen are utilized by the marketing team to generate short-form videos, memes, GIFs, and carousels rapidly to maintain omnichannel omnipresence.
* **Conversational Nurturing:** HighLevel's AI agents manage SMS and email sequences to automatically book leads onto the calendars of the high-ticket sales closers.
* **Brand Reputation (GEO):** Generative Engine Optimization tools, such as the Vibe SEO AI Agent, are used to manage LLM citations and distinguish the legitimate Miami-based business from a fraudulent UK street-charity operating under the name "Inside Success Union".

## 4. Strategic AI Integration Roadmap
* **Post-Production Automation:** Deploying API-driven AI video editing tools triggered via Make.com to automate rough cuts, highlight extraction, and social media clip generation for the ISTV editing team.
* **Sales Floor Analytics:** Integrating cognitive AI platforms into the Zoom stack to transcribe and analyze the 16 daily calls handled by the massive sales department, optimizing objection-handling algorithms.
* **AI-Assisted Scriptwriting:** Training custom LLMs on Typeform intakes and discovery call transcripts to instantly generate structured, Hollywood-style narrative scripts, removing the blank-page bottleneck for the creative team.
